Creation of New Values through Structural Shift

On top of the high-value-added processing of synthetic fibers, microfibers, etc.,
KB SEIREN Nagahama Plant has been promoting structural shift to become a“high-value-added”plant to develop integrated products
using differentiated fibers produced in Hokuriku Synthetic Fibers Plant, with a further view to dyeing, knitting, finishing, down to some finished goods production.
Headed for“company excellence in the 21st century,”which Seiren Group companies aspire for, KB SEIREN is committed to“create new values”in the fields ranging from clothing to non-clothing, in order to play a part in developing new technologies, new products and new markets.
