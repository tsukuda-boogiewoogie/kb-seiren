"Suvin" is a cross between "SUJATA" grown in India and "Sea-Island Cotton" grown in the Caribbean island nation of St. Vincent. Grown only in southern India's Tamil Nadu state. Presently, "Suvin" is the prime and paramount cotton in India.

  • Rare value with annual production of about 4000 bales (680 tons) only
  • Smooth hand and silky touch with natural luster
  • As the staples are very thin compared with other extra long staple cotton, the number of staples in a yarn becomes larger, leading to the higher tenacity of the yarn.

　
　

Features

  • Soft
  • Smooth hand
  • Long staple length and strong tenacity
  • Bright coloration
  • Lustrous
