Features

Eliminates Static on Fibers

Belltron, the electroconductive fiber mixed in Doden, has extremely high capaciity of eliminating electricity, even if its mixture volume is small. Under the low-humidity conditions, friction-caused electrification is suppressed and static on fibers is eliminated.

Precludes Electrification of Human Body

Doden's excellent property to eliminate static on fibers also precludes electrification of a human body, and immediately discharges electricity on it if any. It solves various static problems like when touching a metal door knob or problems an enviroment surrounded by electronic apparatuses causes.

Semipermanent Antistatic Effect

For its sandwich construction, Belltron retains outstanding fastness to washing, flexibility and abrasion resistance, which are not seen in other fibers like coated, metallic or carbon fibers.

Doden Improves Comfortable-to-Wear Property of Polyester

Given the static-eliminating property, Doden further improves the comfortableness of polyester for which it is generally used for apparel, further widening polyester's sphere of activity.

Applications

Coats, suits, dresses, blouses, sportswear, inner wear, linings etc.
