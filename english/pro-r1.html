To Develop Beautiful Shades, whether Light or Deep : A High-Quality Polyester Filament, Cationic-Dyeable at 100℃ under Normal Pressure

Rakusen has finally opened the way for piece-dyeing of polyester at 100℃ under normal pressure, developing beautiful colors in any depth of shade. This feature allows creation of a variety of new products, including mixed fabrics with natural fibers and stretch fabrics by combining polyurethane, while causing no degradation of each material used. Moreover, since the filament is made of polyester chips manufactured in the world’s first continuous direct polymerization system, it has superior strength, elongation and uniformity to similar types of products that are made of polyester chips obtained from the conventional batch process.

Features

  • Cationic-dyeable at 100℃ under normal pressure
  • Facilitates piece-dyeing of mixed fabrics with such fibers as polyurethane, wool, and silk, which are vulnerable to high temperature/pressure
  • Eliminates the risk of sublimation as often seen in the disperse dyeing of regular polyester hybrid fabrics
  • Can be supplied in the form of superfine filaments as well as multifilament yarns
  • Soft and smooth to the touch
  • Possesses a high level of strength, elongation, and uniformity in quality

Applications

Sportswear, inner wear, outer wear, heavy clothing (e.g. jackets, overcoats, winter clothing), accessories etc.
