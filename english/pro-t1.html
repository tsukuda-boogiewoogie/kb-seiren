Compact is an entirely new type of sophisticated material, made by using specialty split-type yarn (65% polyester, 35% nylon) developed by KB SEIREN in both warp and weft. The fabric is densely woven, and is further made to shrink at the company's Nagahama Plant with its unique, advanced technology. Offering a delicate and supple feel, while also possessing high resiliency, Compact paves the way for a new world of outer garments.

Features

  • Delicate to the touch, thanks to special finishing
  • Highly resilient owing to extreme fabric density
  • Shape-retentive to provide rich variety of expressions
  • Water-repellent and moisture-permeable as innate fabric characteristics, not as a result of aftertreatment

Applications

Outer garments in general (blousons, coats, skirts, jackets etc.)
