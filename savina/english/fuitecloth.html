Product Introduction

FUITECLOTH II is a wiping cloth which can be used for all delicate objects from peripheral devices in clean rooms to final products manufactured therein. It is a knitted fabric composed of “Belima” microfiber, and the fabric edges are firmly sealed with an advanced cutting technique to fully minimize its lint generation properties. A special treatment is also applied to the original fiber to improve its water/solvent retention.

Ion exchange water is used in the production process, and the final product is thoroughly cleaned and packaged in class 100 clean rooms using ultrapure water. As a result, dissolution of residual ions is strictly controlled at its lowest possible level.
Superfine microfiber “Belima” consists of wedgy polyester component and cross-shaped nylon component. This structure allows FUITECLOTH II to fit even the most minutely uneven surface to remove dust particles and oil stains reliably. Dust and dirt are entrapped between filaments and will not be released to soil the wiped object again.
