Product Introduction
Savina AS is a combination of ultra-fine microfiber Belima X and conductive fiber Belltron. This product possesses the antistatic properties of Belltron in addition to the high wiper performance of Savina Minimax. It guarantees static-free cleaning of computer peripherals and delicate electronic devices.
